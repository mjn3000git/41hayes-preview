Check-in and check-out

Check-in and check-out times are in your booking confirmation. Entry details are sent privately before you arrive. (Times: Matthew to confirm.)

House tips

  • Trash and recycling: please bag all trash and use the bins outside. Bear-safe habits matter here, so never leave food or trash out overnight. (Pickup day: Matthew to confirm.)
  • Quiet hours: please keep things quiet in the evening and overnight, out of respect for our neighbors. (Hours: Matthew to confirm.)
  • Thermostat: please keep the heat at a comfortable, moderate setting, and turn it down when you head out for the day.
  • Before you leave: lock up, turn off lights, and leave the thermostat at the away setting.

Around town

The house is about 300 feet from Schroon Lake. Beach access nearby is shared and public, so please be kind to other beachgoers and pack out what you bring.
