Request a seasonal or long-term stay

The Hayes House is available for seasonal stays (a month or more) and long-term stays (six months or more), arranged directly with Matthew.

About the stay

41 Hayes Road sits about 300 feet from Schroon Lake, with a short walk to shared public beach access. It is a quiet Adirondack home base for families and friends.

How a request works

  1. Tell us your dates, stay type, guests, and how to reach you.
  2. Matthew reads every request and replies personally.
  3. Once Matthew replies, your requested dates can get a tentative hold on our calendar while we work out the details.
  4. Your stay is booked only when Matthew confirms it with you directly.

Please note: sending a request is not a booking or a confirmation. No payment is taken on this site. Matthew confirms dates personally.
